# Leave and Absence Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility criteria, accrual rates, approval processes, and usage guidelines. It ensures fair and consistent management of employee time off while maintaining business continuity.

## 2. Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)

### 2.1 Accrual Rates
Annual leave accrues from the first day of employment based on tenure:

| Years of Service | Annual Entitlement | Monthly Accrual |
|-----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 0-2 years | 15 days | 1.25 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days | 1.67 days |
| 6-10 years | 25 days | 2.08 days |
| 10+ years | 30 days | 2.50 days |

### 2.2 Usage Rules
- Minimum leave request is 0.5 days (half day)
- Maximum consecutive leave is 15 working days without VP approval
- Leave exceeding 15 consecutive days requires VP-level approval submitted 30 days in advance
- Leave requests of 3 or more days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance
- Leave requests of 1-2 days must be submitted at least 3 business days in advance

### 2.3 Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused leave days to the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by March 31 of the following year or they expire. Unused leave beyond the carry-forward limit is forfeited and will not be compensated.

### 2.4 Leave Encashment
Employees with more than 3 years of service may encash up to 5 days of unused annual leave per year. Encashment is calculated at the employee's basic daily rate. Encashment requests must be submitted during November and will be paid in December.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement
All employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not accrue and is available from the first day of employment.

### 3.2 Reporting Requirements
- Employees must notify their manager by 9:30 AM on the day of absence
- Notification can be made by phone, email, or messaging platform
- For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a licensed physician is required
- The medical certificate must be submitted to HR within 2 business days of returning to work

### 3.3 Extended Illness
For illnesses exceeding 12 days in a calendar year:
- Days 13-30: Paid at 75% of base salary
- Days 31-60: Paid at 50% of base salary
- Beyond 60 days: Unpaid leave; long-term disability insurance may apply

## 4. Personal Leave

Employees receive 3 days of personal leave per calendar year for personal matters such as moving house, attending legal proceedings, or family emergencies. Personal leave does not accrue or carry forward.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to bereavement leave as follows:
- Spouse, child, or parent: 5 working days
- Sibling, grandparent, or in-law: 3 working days
- Extended family or close friend: 1 working day

Bereavement leave is paid at full salary and does not count against annual leave.

## 6. Parental Leave

### 6.1 Maternity Leave
Female employees who have completed 6 months of service are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. This can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date. Maternity leave is paid at full salary for the first 16 weeks and 80% salary for the remaining 10 weeks.

### 6.2 Paternity Leave
Male employees who have completed 6 months of service are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption date. Paternity leave is paid at full salary.

### 6.3 Adoption Leave
Employees adopting a child under the age of 5 receive the same leave entitlements as biological parents. Documentation from the adoption agency is required.

## 7. Public Holidays

The company observes 10 public holidays per calendar year. The list of observed holidays is published by HR in December for the following year. Employees required to work on public holidays receive compensatory time off (1.5 days per holiday worked) to be taken within 60 days.

## 8. Leave Without Pay

### 8.1 Eligibility
Employees who have exhausted all paid leave may request leave without pay (LWOP). LWOP must be approved by the employee's VP and HR. LWOP in excess of 30 days in a rolling 12-month period may affect benefits eligibility and tenure calculations.

### 8.2 Impact on Benefits
During unpaid leave:
- Health insurance continues for the first 30 days at company expense
- Beyond 30 days, the employee must pay the full premium to maintain coverage
- Retirement contributions are suspended
- Leave accrual is suspended

## 9. Leave During Notice Period

Employees in their notice period may not take more than 3 days of leave without explicit HR approval. Unapproved absences during the notice period will be treated as leave without pay.

## 10. Abuse of Leave Policy

Patterns of leave abuse such as frequent Monday/Friday absences, repeated unexplained sick leave, or leave taken without approval may result in:

1. Verbal warning from the manager
2. Written warning from HR
3. Suspension of flexible working arrangements
4. Formal disciplinary proceedings

All leave records are maintained for 7 years and may be audited.
