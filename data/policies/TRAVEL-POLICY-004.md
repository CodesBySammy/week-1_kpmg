# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs all business travel arrangements for Meridian Insurance Group employees. It ensures cost-effective travel planning, employee safety, and proper documentation of travel expenses.

## 2. Travel Authorization

### 2.1 Pre-Approval Requirements
All business travel must be pre-approved before booking:
- Domestic travel: Manager approval required at least 7 days before departure
- International travel: VP approval required at least 21 days before departure
- Emergency travel: Manager verbal approval followed by written confirmation within 24 hours

### 2.2 Travel Request
Travel requests must include:
- Business justification and expected outcomes
- Destination and duration
- Estimated total cost
- Whether client-billable
- Alternative options considered (e.g., video conference instead of travel)

## 3. Air Travel

### 3.1 Booking Class
- Domestic flights under 4 hours: Economy class
- Domestic flights over 4 hours: Premium economy (if available)
- International flights under 8 hours: Premium economy
- International flights over 8 hours: Business class
- First class is not permitted unless medically necessary with documented physician recommendation

### 3.2 Advance Booking
Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance to secure lower fares. Bookings made less than 7 days before departure require Director approval regardless of amount.

### 3.3 Airline Selection
Employees should select the most cost-effective option among approved carriers. Loyalty program preferences are permitted if the fare difference is within 10% of the lowest available fare.

### 3.4 Baggage
Standard checked baggage fees are reimbursable. Excess or overweight baggage fees are reimbursable only when transporting company equipment or materials.

## 4. Ground Transportation

### 4.1 Rental Cars
- Standard or intermediate class vehicles are the default
- Full-size or SUV vehicles require justification (e.g., multiple passengers, equipment transport)
- Luxury and specialty vehicles are not permitted
- Fuel costs for rental vehicles are reimbursable with receipts
- Rental insurance should be declined when using a company credit card that provides coverage

### 4.2 Taxi and Ride-Share
Taxi and ride-share services (Uber, Lyft) are reimbursable for business travel. Ride-share is preferred over taxis where available. Receipts are required for all rides over $15.

### 4.3 Personal Vehicle
Employees using personal vehicles for business travel are reimbursed at the current IRS mileage rate ($0.67 per mile as of 2025). A mileage log documenting start location, destination, purpose, and distance is required.

### 4.4 Parking and Tolls
Parking fees and road tolls incurred during business travel are reimbursable with receipts. Airport parking for trips exceeding 3 days should use economy parking lots rather than terminal parking.

## 5. Accommodation

### 5.1 Hotel Standards
- Standard room at a mid-range business hotel (e.g., Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt)
- Maximum nightly rate by city tier:

| City Tier | Maximum Rate |
|-----------|-------------|
| Tier 1 (NYC, SF, DC, London) | $300/night |
| Tier 2 (Chicago, Boston, LA) | $250/night |
| Tier 3 (Other major cities) | $200/night |
| Tier 4 (Smaller cities/suburbs) | $150/night |

### 5.2 Extended Stay
For business travel exceeding 5 consecutive nights, employees should consider extended-stay hotels or serviced apartments which typically offer lower nightly rates and kitchen facilities.

### 5.3 Sharing
Employees are never required to share hotel rooms. Each traveler receives their own room.

## 6. Per-Diem Rates

### 6.1 Meal Per-Diem
When traveling on business, employees receive meal per-diem instead of itemized meal reimbursement:

| City Tier | Daily Meal Allowance |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Tier 1 | $85/day |
| Tier 2 | $70/day |
| Tier 3 | $60/day |
| Tier 4 | $50/day |

Per-diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidental expenses. No receipts required for per-diem claims.

### 6.2 Partial Day Travel
- Departure day: 75% of daily per-diem
- Return day: 75% of daily per-diem
- Days with provided meals (e.g., conference lunch): Reduce by $15 per provided meal

## 7. International Travel

### 7.1 Additional Requirements
International travelers must:
- Verify passport validity (minimum 6 months beyond travel dates)
- Obtain required visas and permits (company covers costs)
- Register travel with the company's travel security provider
- Carry a copy of the company's international emergency contacts
- Verify mobile phone international plan activation

### 7.2 Foreign Currency
- Company credit cards should be used for all international expenses
- Cash advances in foreign currency are available through Finance with 5 business days notice
- Exchange rate used for reimbursement is the rate on the date of the transaction

### 7.3 Travel Insurance
The company provides comprehensive travel insurance for all international business trips. Coverage includes:
- Medical expenses up to $500,000
- Emergency evacuation
- Trip cancellation and interruption
- Lost luggage replacement up to $3,000

## 8. Safety and Security

### 8.1 Travel Advisories
Travel to countries or regions with active government travel advisories requires:
- VP and HR approval
- Security briefing from the company's security team
- Registration with the nearest embassy or consulate
- Daily check-in protocol with the designated contact

### 8.2 Emergency Procedures
In case of emergency during business travel:
1. Contact local emergency services (911 or local equivalent)
2. Contact the company's 24/7 travel emergency line: +1-800-555-HELP
3. Notify your manager as soon as safely possible
4. Follow instructions from the travel security provider

## 9. Travel Expense Reporting

All travel expenses must be submitted within 14 days of return. The expense report must include:
- Pre-approval reference number
- Itemized expenses with receipts
- Per-diem claims by day
- Business purpose documentation
- Client billing codes if applicable

Late submissions (beyond 30 days) require Director-level justification.
