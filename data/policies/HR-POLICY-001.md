# Human Resources General Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the general human resources guidelines for all employees of Meridian Insurance Group. It applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees across all departments and office locations.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure consistent, fair, and legally compliant treatment of all employees. Every manager and team lead is responsible for understanding and enforcing these guidelines within their teams.

## 2. Employment Classifications

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees
Full-time employees work a minimum of 40 hours per week and are eligible for the complete benefits package including health insurance, retirement contributions, paid time off, and professional development allowances.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees
Part-time employees work fewer than 30 hours per week. They receive prorated benefits based on their scheduled hours. Part-time employees working more than 20 hours per week are eligible for health insurance coverage at a 50% employer contribution rate.

### 2.3 Contract Employees
Contract employees are engaged for a specific project or time period. They are not eligible for company benefits unless explicitly stated in their contract. Contract duration must not exceed 18 months without a formal review and renewal process.

## 3. Probationary Period

All new employees undergo a 90-day probationary period starting from their date of hire. During this period:

- Performance reviews are conducted at 30, 60, and 90 days
- Termination can occur with 7 days notice (rather than the standard 30 days)
- Employees are not eligible for performance bonuses
- Health insurance coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment

Successful completion of the probationary period is confirmed in writing by the employee's direct manager and the HR department.

## 4. Working Hours and Attendance

### 4.1 Standard Working Hours
The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours during which all employees must be available are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

### 4.2 Flexible Working
Employees who have completed their probationary period may request flexible working arrangements. Requests must be submitted to their manager and approved by HR. Flexible arrangements include:

- Adjusted start/end times (within the range of 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM)
- Compressed work weeks (four 10-hour days)
- Remote work (maximum 2 days per week for most roles)

### 4.3 Overtime
Non-exempt employees who work beyond 40 hours per week are entitled to overtime compensation at 1.5 times their regular hourly rate. All overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's manager. Unauthorized overtime may result in disciplinary action.

## 5. Code of Conduct

### 5.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior in the workplace and when representing the company. This includes:

- Treating colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and courtesy
- Maintaining confidentiality of sensitive business and client information
- Avoiding conflicts of interest
- Reporting ethical concerns through the appropriate channels

### 5.2 Dress Code
Business casual attire is the standard dress code for office-based employees. Client-facing meetings may require formal business attire. Specific department guidelines may apply for field and operations staff.

### 5.3 Anti-Harassment
Meridian Insurance Group maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment of any kind, including sexual harassment, bullying, and discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, or sexual orientation. All complaints must be reported to HR and will be investigated within 5 business days.

## 6. Performance Management

### 6.1 Annual Reviews
All employees receive a formal performance review annually, conducted during Q4 (October-December). Reviews assess:

- Achievement of objectives set during the previous review
- Core competency demonstration
- Professional development progress
- Contribution to team and company goals

### 6.2 Performance Improvement Plans
Employees not meeting performance expectations may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP lasts 60 days and includes:

- Specific, measurable improvement targets
- Weekly check-ins with the manager
- Access to additional training or mentoring
- Clear consequences if targets are not met

### 6.3 Compensation Review
Salary adjustments are reviewed annually in conjunction with performance reviews. The standard merit increase range is 3-7% based on performance rating. Exceptional performance may qualify for increases up to 10% with VP-level approval.

## 7. Grievance Procedure

Employees who wish to raise a formal grievance should follow this process:

1. **Step 1:** Discuss the concern with their direct manager within 10 business days of the incident
2. **Step 2:** If unresolved, submit a written grievance to HR within 5 business days
3. **Step 3:** HR will investigate and respond within 15 business days
4. **Step 4:** If the employee disagrees with the outcome, they may appeal to the VP of HR within 10 business days
5. **Step 5:** The VP's decision is final and will be communicated within 10 business days

## 8. Termination and Exit

### 8.1 Voluntary Resignation
Employees must provide a minimum of 30 days written notice of resignation. Senior managers and directors must provide 60 days notice. During the notice period, employees are expected to complete knowledge transfer and transition responsibilities.

### 8.2 Involuntary Termination
Involuntary termination may occur due to poor performance, misconduct, redundancy, or business restructuring. Except in cases of gross misconduct, employees will receive:

- 30 days notice or pay in lieu
- Severance pay based on tenure (1 week per year of service, up to 26 weeks)
- Outplacement support for employees with 2+ years of service

### 8.3 Exit Interview
All departing employees are invited to participate in a confidential exit interview with HR. The purpose is to gather feedback for continuous improvement of workplace policies and culture.
