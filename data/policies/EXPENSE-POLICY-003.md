# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for business expense reimbursement at Meridian Insurance Group. All employees incurring business-related expenses must comply with these guidelines to ensure timely reimbursement and accurate financial reporting.

## 2. General Principles

- All business expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to company business
- Pre-approval is required for any single expense exceeding $250
- Receipts are mandatory for all expenses over $25
- Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense
- Reimbursement is processed within 15 business days of approved submission
- Personal expenses are never reimbursable, even if incurred during business activities

## 3. Meals and Entertainment

### 3.1 Client Meals
Meals with clients or business partners are reimbursable within the following limits:

| Meal Type | Per Person Limit |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Breakfast | $25 |
| Lunch | $40 |
| Dinner | $75 |

Alcohol may be reimbursed up to $30 per person when dining with clients. The names and business affiliations of all attendees must be documented on the expense report.

### 3.2 Team Meals
Manager-approved team meals are reimbursable up to $35 per person. Team meals require at least 3 team members and must have a documented business purpose such as a project milestone celebration, team building, or working lunch during extended projects.

### 3.3 Individual Meals
Individual meals while traveling on business are reimbursable within the per-diem rates specified in the Travel Policy (TRAVEL-POLICY-004). Individual meals at the regular office location are not reimbursable.

## 4. Office Supplies and Equipment

### 4.1 Standard Supplies
Office supplies up to $50 per month may be purchased without pre-approval. This includes pens, notebooks, printer paper, and similar consumables. Receipts must be retained and submitted monthly.

### 4.2 Technology and Equipment
Technology purchases require pre-approval regardless of amount:
- Keyboards, mice, and accessories under $100: Manager approval
- Monitors, headsets, and peripherals $100-$500: Director approval
- Laptops, tablets, and equipment over $500: VP approval and IT coordination

All technology purchases become company property and must be returned upon departure.

## 5. Professional Development

### 5.1 Training and Courses
Employees may expense training courses, certifications, and educational materials up to $2,000 per fiscal year with manager approval. Expenses exceeding $2,000 require VP approval and a written justification.

### 5.2 Conferences
Conference attendance including registration, travel, and accommodation is reimbursable with Director approval. The employee must submit a post-conference summary within 10 business days.

### 5.3 Professional Memberships
Annual professional memberships directly relevant to the employee's role are reimbursable up to $500 per year. Examples include IEEE, ACM, or industry-specific associations.

## 6. Communication Expenses

### 6.1 Mobile Phone
Employees required to use personal mobile phones for business purposes receive a monthly allowance of $75. This applies to employees who are on call, frequently travel, or need mobile access for client communication.

### 6.2 Home Internet
Remote workers approved for regular work-from-home arrangements receive a monthly internet allowance of $50.

## 7. Submission Process

### 7.1 Required Documentation
Every expense report must include:
1. Date of expense
2. Vendor or merchant name
3. Business purpose and justification
4. Names of attendees (for meals and entertainment)
5. Project or cost center code
6. Original receipt or electronic receipt image

### 7.2 Approval Chain
| Expense Amount | Approval Required |
|---------------|------------------|
| Under $100 | Automatic (with receipt) |
| $100-$500 | Manager approval |
| $500-$2,000 | Director approval |
| $2,000-$5,000 | VP approval |
| Over $5,000 | CFO approval |

### 7.3 Deadlines
- Expense reports more than 30 days old require manager justification for late submission
- Expense reports more than 90 days old will not be reimbursed except with CFO exception approval
- End-of-quarter expenses must be submitted within 5 business days of quarter close

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are explicitly not reimbursable:
- Personal entertainment or recreation
- Traffic fines or parking tickets
- Personal grooming or clothing (unless required uniforms)
- Gifts for colleagues exceeding $25 in value
- Political or charitable donations
- First-class travel upgrades (unless medically required)
- Mini-bar charges at hotels
- In-room movies
- Gym or spa services
- Lost or stolen personal property

## 9. Fraud and Misuse

Submitting false, inflated, or duplicate expense claims constitutes fraud. Consequences include:
- Immediate repayment of the fraudulent amount
- Formal disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Potential criminal prosecution for claims exceeding $1,000
- Reporting to industry regulatory bodies where applicable

All expense records are subject to random audit by the Finance team. Approximately 20% of submissions are selected for detailed review each quarter.
