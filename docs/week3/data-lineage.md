# End-to-End Data Lineage & Provenance

## 1. Traceability Chain
Every answer generated by the platform can be audited back to source files:
$$\text{Generated Answer} \longrightarrow \text{Citation} \longrightarrow \text{Chunk ID} \longrightarrow \text{Raw Policy Document}$$

Each chunk carries full provenance metadata: `document_id`, `section`, `chunk_id`, `strategy`, and `source_file`.
